02 — Industry experience

Capstone

Security Advisor

IDcare — Australia & New Zealand's national identity and cyber support service

Served as Security Advisor on an R&D project researching social engineering and scam tactics across Facebook and Instagram, then developing practical account recovery plans and mitigation strategies for victims of online fraud.

Working with a national support service meant the output had to be usable by people already in distress, not just technically correct. Recovery guidance that assumes a calm, technically literate reader fails exactly when it's needed. That constraint shaped how I write security guidance now.
